<section class="intro-section">
  <p class="lead">
    I am a first-year CS Ph.D. student at UC Berkeley, where I am part of
    <a href="https://bair.berkeley.edu/">BAIR</a> and the
    <a href="https://sky.cs.berkeley.edu/">Sky Computing Lab</a>.
